1. Chemical Equilibrium & Henderson-Hasselbalch Equation

  • Relation between Kp and Kc: Kp = Kc · (R · T)^(Δn_g)
  • Acidic Buffer pH: pH = pKa + log₁₀([Conjugate Base] / [Weak Acid])
  • Solubility Product (AgCl): Ksp = [Ag⁺][Cl⁻] = s²
