1. Eccentricity, Latus Rectum & Slope-Form Tangents

  • Parabola (y² = 4ax): LR = 4a, Tangent of slope m: y = mx + a/m
  • Ellipse (x²/a² + y²/b² = 1): b² = a²(1 − e²), LR = 2b²/a, Tangent: y = mx ± √(a²m² + b²)
  • Hyperbola (x²/a² − y²/b² = 1): b² = a²(e² − 1), Tangent: y = mx ± √(a²m² − b²)
